## My favourite math formulas
Here are some of my favourite math formulas:

$$
y = \alpha x^2 + \beta x + \gamma
$$

,- this is the parabolic function formula: $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ are coefficients, and $x$ denotes input variable;

$$
S = \pi R^2
$$

,- this is the area of a circle formula: $S$ denotes area, $\pi$ is the mathematical constant pi, and $R$ is the radius;

$$
F = \mu \cdot \rho \cdot g \cdot \lambda \cdot \tau
$$

,- this is the friction force formula for a sliding block: $F$ denotes friction force, $\mu$ is the friction coefficient, $\rho$ is material density, $g$ is gravitational acceleration, $\lambda$ denotes block thickness, and $\tau$ is the contact area;

$$
y = \frac{k_1}{k_2x + c}
$$

,- this is the modified hyperbolic function: $k_1,k_2$ denote coefficients, $x$ - input variable, $c$ - constant value;

$$
D = b^2 - 4ac, x_1 = \frac{-b+\sqrt D}{2a}, x_2 = \frac{-b-\sqrt D}{2a}
$$

,- this is how to solve quadratic equation: $D$ is discriminant, $a, b, c$ denote equation's coefficients, $x_1, x_2$ denote the solutions;

$$
\begin{cases}
2x+3y-z=7\\
4x-y+2z=4\\
-x+2y+5x=11
\end{cases}
$$

,- this is a system of linear equations: $x, y, z$ denote the unknown variables;

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

,- this is a formula for finding the sum of first $N$ positive integers;

$$
I_n = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & \cdots & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

,- this is an identity matrix of rank $N$.
